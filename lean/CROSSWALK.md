## Technical choices, not a change of argument

1. **Specialization.** The paper permits any fixed ε in (0, 1/3). We use the same argument at 1/4 as requested, rather than claim to formalize the whole family of hypotheses. Fourth powers convert (1) to `c⁴≤K rad(abc)⁵`. On the finite exception set choose `K=1+∑c⁴`; outside use its complement. Taking the fourth root or rounding a real fourth power upward gives the exact equivalence proved in `QuarterEquiv.lean`.
2. **Indices instead of angles.** Increasing angle in the first octant is decreasing d; positivity and `aᵢ²+bᵢ²=S²` then give the opposite order for b. The Lean proof uses this direct order. The pigeonhole allows the slightly larger range of size `2N` (rather than `2N−1`), and still gives five pairs. The arithmetic predicate `25N<m²` represents `m>5 sqrt N`.
3. **Gaussian arithmetic.** Mathlib `GaussianInt=Zsqrtd(-1)`, `star` is conjugation, and `norm` is squared absolute value. `Associated` means up to a unit. `multiplicity` and `Nat.factorization` are the genuine Gaussian and rational prime exponents. At odd p either p is a Gaussian prime or it has a norm-p Gaussian factor; that factor and its conjugate are distinct, proved by a norm argument. The tie in the nearest-endpoint definition always goes to zero.
4. **Clear fractions without losing information.** Halves in (7) are represented by twice each valuation. Let `X′=X/H`, `D=D₀`. The preprint's reduced rational `t=X′/P²` has every prime exponent between `−v(D)` and `v(D)`; equivalently `T=D X′/P²` is an integer dividing `D²`. Lean proves the latter and the coefficient is exactly `A T²`. In applying abc the gcd of the three quartic terms already divides `c₁c₂` by coprimality of the first two P's; multiplying by the third coefficient gives the bound used in the paper.
5. **Retain the real logarithmic cut argument.** For a pair, the needed side of the log estimate is obtained by proving `K² | W² dᵢ dⱼ` prime by prime, with W the product of the separating prime powers. Taking logs gives `log W≥log K−(log dᵢ+log dⱼ)/2`. The global proof uses the same log weights and (10). Finite label incidences on 5 vertices and the 3 pairings are checked by kernel reduction via `decide`, not by an external solver or a native-decision axiom. The explicit exponent is `15+30·540=16215`.
6. **Same final divisor argument.** For fixed positive integer L, absorb the finitely many primes below `2^L` into the constant bounding `(2^ω)ᴸ/q`. Use L=2 for q≤N and L=2(B+1) for larger q. This is the preprint's small-η argument with the denominator cleared. The explicit constants are not advertised as practical or effectively known from an unspecified abc constant.
7. **Precise upstream match.** The integer count includes zero and the intermediate positive count does not. The pinned DeepMind definitions count natural nontrivial progressions; their exact definition copies are extracted from SHA-256-checked upstream sources, and the kernel checks definitional equality of the ABC premise and Qmax conclusion. No open-conjecture proof from upstream discharges a hypothesis.

No logical divergence from the proof of the ordinary Rudin implication at ε=1/4 was needed. The stronger maximizer conjecture and the truth of classical abc are not claimed.
